01 · Investment principles

Why diversify if KWSP already invests for you?

KWSP itself is diversified. An additional approved unit trust may offer a different portfolio mandate, manager and equity-market exposure.

KWSP: the foundation

A retirement-focused portfolio managed across asset classes, with annual dividend distributions subject to applicable rules.

Unit trust: an optional allocation

Direct exposure to a selected fund's strategy, with potential capital growth and the possibility of substantial losses.

Diversification does not eliminate losses, and a new fund may overlap with investments KWSP already holds.

05 · Risks and costs

Know the trade-offs before investing.

Sales charge

Up to 3%Through agent/counter; i-Akaun transactions may have lower charges, up to 0.5%. Check the actual channel.

Management fee

1.65% p.a.Plus trustee fee 0.06% p.a., and any applicable expenses

Opportunity cost

Amounts invested outside KWSP stop earning KWSP dividends while outside the fund.

Market and liquidity risk

Loss of capital is possible. Redemptions before EPF withdrawal eligibility normally go back to KWSP.

A positive fund return is not automatically a better outcome after fees, missed KWSP dividends and risk.
