01 / The philosophy02 / 09

Diversification is a choice.
Not a return competition.

KWSP already invests across asset classes. An additional unit trust is not automatically “more diversified”—its value depends on what exposure it adds and whether the investor can bear the risk.

Your foundation

KWSP / EPF

A retirement savings system with a diversified portfolio, subject to KWSP's investment decisions, dividend policies and withdrawal rules.

An optional allocation

EPF Member Investment Scheme

Eligible savings can be directed to approved funds with different managers and investment mandates. Market losses and extra charges are borne by the investor.

✦Good diversification asks: Does the fund add a useful strategy—or simply more equity and geographic risk you already have?
Source: KWSP i-InvestDifferent exposure ≠ guaranteed better outcomes

Why the numbers differ

The PHS divides the 5-year total return by five. A CAGR instead describes the constant compounded rate matching the same beginning and ending value. The calculator uses CAGR, not the PHS annual-average figure.

!Past performance is not predictive. Repeating the historical CAGR for 10 years is only one mathematical thought experiment, not a fund return forecast.
