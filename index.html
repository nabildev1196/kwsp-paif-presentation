A PERSPECTIVE WORTH KEEPING

The best plan is one
you can stay with.

01
Protect your foundation.

Keep accessible emergency savings. Only invest money whose time horizon and risk you can accept.

02
Let the route match the goal.

Cash offers flexibility. KWSP transfers remain retirement money. PRS has withdrawal restrictions; general early withdrawals can incur 8% tax.

03
Look beyond the winning number.

Compare fees, drawdowns, mandates and overlapping holdings. A strong decade is evidence to examine, not a promise.
